# ASA DataFest 2026: Best Insight Winner

🏆 Our team, DataVision Avengers, received the **Best Insight** award at ASA DataFest 2026, hosted at the University of Notre Dame.

## Overview

During this 48-hour data analytics competition, our team explored healthcare patient journey data from Northeast Kansas.

Our final project examined over 390,000 respiratory healthcare encounters, focusing on emergency department reliance across patient populations. We combined exploratory data analysis and geospatial visualization to investigate patterns in care utilization and potential disparities in access.

## What Our Team Explored

- Respiratory emergency department reliance by race and age
- Healthcare encounter patterns over time
- Geographic differences across Northeast Kansas
- Socioeconomic and environmental context
- Potential influences of smoking status, seasonality, and time trends

## My Contribution

I served as the team lead, helping coordinate our work and guide the project’s direction. I advised teammates on analytical approaches, discussed emerging findings, and helped prioritize insights for the final presentation.

I also conducted individual exploratory analysis in Google Colab using a teammate’s prepared Parquet file. My notebook explored healthcare encounter characteristics and trends in obstetric and respiratory encounters. The team ultimately used a teammate’s analysis for the final submission.
